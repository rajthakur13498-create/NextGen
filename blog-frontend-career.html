How to start a frontend career in 2026

Build your first steps with clear skills, real projects and simple portfolio goals.

Frontend development is one of the easiest ways to begin a tech career because you can see your work immediately. A good path is to learn HTML, CSS and JavaScript first, then move into React once you are comfortable building simple layouts.

1. Learn the fundamentals

Start with structure, styling and interactivity. Make sure you understand how pages are laid out, how styles are applied and how events work in the browser.

2. Build small real projects

Create a landing page, a calculator, a portfolio website or a small product page. These projects help you turn theory into skill.

One strong portfolio project is better than ten unfinished tutorials.

3. Practice consistently

Spend a little time every day coding. Focus on one concept at a time and keep your projects simple and complete.

4. Prepare your portfolio

Put your work on GitHub and make a simple website that shows your best projects. This gives you something real to share with employers.
